The **Send Itinerary** feature allows agents to send and share itineraries to other agents or agencies via email.

<Image align="center" alt="SendItinerary.png" width="600px" src="https://files.readme.io/b9dec80863fc6e92e2a7ff0144d58306ae87dc3e2bcc94aeb0d5dc6b5e570871-SendItinerary.png" />

## Prerequisites

* Before using the **Send Itinerary** feature, ensure the **Send Itinerary** feature is enabled on the **Features and Permissions** section of the POS used when the booking was made.

## Enable the Send Itinerary feature for a POS

Follow the steps below to enable the Send Itinerary feature for a POS:

* On the ‘Portal’ page, click the **Platform** menu button.

<Image align="center" width="200px" src="https://files.readme.io/cd48b6086894bb717d58768a02150e9560ceb1b55d5e4ef79c3bfbf11251ed09-FOPExport001.png" />

* Click **List Point of Sale** to open the **List POS (Point of Sale)** page.

<Image alt="VoidPaxOnly001.png" width="600px" src="https://files.readme.io/2d506d1be5bb74312b81c516a3f858e2f687ecdbad787b5efeb7ba8c80486cc3-VoidPaxOnly001_1.png" />

* Click the **Edit** icon in the **Action** column to edit the POS you want to enable the **Send Itinerary** feature for.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/516e74c69600d356f699906270f513bb3eaddaacb660e21b34b4cbc81f1a55d4-SendItinerary001.png" />

* Check the **Send Itinerary** checkbox in the **Features and Permissions** section, and click **Update**.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/2776021789f6d0c08bd7cefba9c0546fc7948e226edede6acd228392e99d0959-SendItinerary003.png" />

* The **Send Itinerary** feature is now enabled for the required POS.

## Use the Send Itinerary Feature

Follow the steps below to use the **Send Itinerary** feature for a booking in the **Order Management** page.

* On the ‘Portal’ page, click the **Order Management** option.

<Image align="center" width="50px" src="https://files.readme.io/be47cc35ce9119e1a4b775ee88ce31b2cd0ab3639345045d7d2fbd5d2b4b70f4-ImportPNRScreen002.png" />

* Search for the required booking and click the **Booking Key** or View icon in the **Action** column.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/1490be265c903a8ff64f79fd60887d36d516a6e60d3247a1a1d2b9dcf295410f-SendItinerary004.png" />

* The **Order Details** page of the booking displays.
* Click **Send Itinerary** in the **Trip Details** section of the **Order Details** page.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/596b1a2a6168820d0fff25a04b31e0d96f7f1e96c5ff8abdee841ed602d02ca4-SendItinerary005.png" />

* The **Send Itinerary** page displays.

<Image align="center" width="600px" src="https://files.readme.io/1ae1f239b2e83869abc20bdf37f1b490834b896542c8a1ce301e770b75e40bfb-SendItinerary.png" />

* Enter the required **Email ID(s)**, **Message**, check **Without Price** if required, and then click **Send**.

<Callout theme="default">
  **Note:** Checking **Without Price**, removes the price value in the sent itinerary.
</Callout>

<Image align="center" alt="SendItinerary006.png" width="600px" src="https://files.readme.io/6f088bf0cc3155422d592a5030e1b4c7a71533ba18835af25c70b386cf795efb-SendItinerary006.png" />

* The email is sent, and the following message displays. Click **OK**.

<Image align="center" width="600px" src="https://files.readme.io/6a4d6eed24cd0b21d6adcdc46de3d03c6c41aa1f7ffe1891c183e254740a9a3b-SendItinerary007.png" />

* The sent itinerary is delivered to the specified **Email ID(s)**.
* Open the attached **PDF** to view the sent itinerary.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/fb64550189c9438878dd0a9287509c5fd9373152b2008a4aa792c9f0dc438d2a-SendItinerary008.png" />

* The sent itinerary PDF file opens.

<Callout theme="default">
  **Note:** The sent itinerary doesn’t contain the price of the booking because the **Without Price** checkbox was checked. Uncheck the **Without Price** checkbox to include the itinerary’s price when using the **Send Itinerary** feature.
</Callout>
